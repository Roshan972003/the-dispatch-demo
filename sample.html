01Fintech
RBI opens AA network, but incumbents keep the pipes
Shreya’s take
“
Mandating interoperability across consent managers levels the plumbing, but it will not redistribute the data volume. Incumbent account aggregators already own the institutional integrations with banks and lenders, so smaller players inherit equal access without equal distribution. Watch whether the largest players lose actual query volume or simply pick up everyone else's traffic.

02Climate & Mobility
The economics behind Yulu’s fleet expansion
Shreya’s take
“
Doubling fleet size to 100,000 EVs while chasing EBIT breakeven means absorbing severe asset depreciation upfront. Adding up to 11,000 scooters a month works only if daily gig worker rental demand matches that rapid rollout immediately. Watch the vehicle utilisation rates as deliveries accelerate, because unrented hardware compounds losses faster than scale hides them.

03Fintech
PhysicsWallah’s FinZ sells Rs 95.8 Cr loan book to Auxilo
Shreya’s take
“
Running an in-house NBFC sounds neat until public market investors realise student defaults sit directly on your balance sheet. Offloading a Rs 95.8 crore loan portfolio to Auxilo shows PhysicsWallah choosing third-party fee distribution over holding education credit risk. The real signal is that building captive lending is far costlier than just partnering with lenders who already know how to price it.

04Consumer & D2C
Will PB Fintech’s healthcare push help Policybazaar?
Shreya’s take
“
Pouring Rs 10,000 crore into physical hospitals turns an asset-light digital broker into a capital-heavy brick-and-mortar operator. A targeted Rs 500 crore revenue run rate by March 2027 shows how slow the return will be relative to that outlay. Watch whether hospital overhead drags down the group long before any synergy reaches Policybazaar.

05Markets & IPOs
ETSA 2026: Indian founders eyeing early IPOs instead of private capital: Meesho CEO Vidit Aatrey
Shreya’s take
“
Targeting public markets early is an admission about private capital, not just a preference. Contrasting India with the US masks the real incentive: domestic public markets offer liquidity when late-stage private checks dry up or demand brutal governance terms. Watch which founders actually list, and who uses the talk to force better private valuations.

06Markets & IPOs
Zetwerk raises fresh issue size to Rs 2,830 Cr, OFS component up 49% in RHP
Shreya’s take
“
A 49% jump in the secondary component shows existing backers taking meaningful cash off the table before public markets price the stock. Of the Rs 2,830 crore fresh capital, Rs 1,300 crore goes straight to parent borrowings and Rs 860 crore clears subsidiary debt. Public equity is essentially being swapped in to clean up leverage.

07Layoffs & Workforce
Flipkart's former employees petition to include 20,000-30,000 of group's ex-staff in ESOP buyout: Report
Shreya’s take
“
ESOP buybacks exist to keep current talent from leaving, not to reward loyalty that already walked out the door. Walmart has little operational incentive to divert part of a $2 billion pool to former staff when the public listing can absorb that paper later. The petition is less about fairness and more about how indefinite that IPO timeline really is.

08Consumer & D2C
India’s regional D2C food brands turn to physical tastings as online customer acquisition costs rise
Shreya’s take
“
Physical sampling is treated as a low-tech growth hack, but it trades variable ad clicks for fixed promoter and inventory expenses. Handing out samples forces brands to pay upfront for footfall they cannot retarget if conversion stalls. Watch repeat purchase rates outside the tasting venue, because trial is cheap while loyalty remains expensive.

09Funding & Deals
Govt selects 19 fund managers under RDI, committing Rs 7,131 crore
Shreya’s take
“
A fund-of-funds commitment is an allocation of intent, not dry powder ready on day one. Handing Rs 7,131 crore across 19 fund managers means these intermediaries still have to go out and raise the private matching capital before any startup sees a rupee. Watch how quickly the first closes actually happen.

10SaaS & Enterprise
B2B ecom platform Udaan targets IPO by early 2028 as turnaround takes hold
Shreya’s take
“
Targeting an IPO in 2028 based on 10 to 12 percent growth makes this a restructuring story, not an expansion play.
